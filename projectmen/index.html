NETWORK DEFENSE & HONEYPOT SYSTEM_

Three virtual servers, one hardened perimeter. VM2 runs the WordPress site and the Mail Server; anyone who tries to SSH into it is silently redirected into VM1 — the Honeypot. Real admins only get in through OpenVPN + port knocking, while VM3 keeps the backups offline.
